How it works

  1. Pick a character frame (or none) for your strip.
  2. Optionally add pose inspo to reference while you shoot.
  3. Set your countdown time, then hit the shutter.
  4. Line your face up in the hole — we'll take 8 photos back to back.
  5. Pick your favorite 4, tapping them in the order you want on the strip.
  6. Enter an email or phone number and we'll get your strip ready.
