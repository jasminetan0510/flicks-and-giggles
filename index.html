How it works

  1. Pick a countdown length and, if you like, a pose-inspo strip to shoot alongside.
  2. Hit the shutter — we'll take 8 photos back to back, with a beep for the last 3 seconds of each countdown.
  3. Pick your favorite 4, tapping them in the order you want on the strip.
  4. Drag stickers onto your finished strip to decorate it — drag the corner handle to resize, or tap × to remove.
  5. Pick a strip background color if you'd like one.
  6. Enter an email or phone number and we'll get your strip ready.
